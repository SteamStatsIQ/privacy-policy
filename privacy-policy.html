Privacy Policy

At Steam Stats IQ, your privacy is our priority. This extension does not collect, store, or share any personal data or browsing information. We believe in transparency and respect your right to privacy.

All data retrieved by this extension, such as game statistics, is fetched directly from publicly available APIs, including Steam and third-party services. This data is processed locally on your device and is not transmitted to any external servers controlled by us.

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please feel free to reach out to us at steamstatsiq@gmail.com.
